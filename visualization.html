Heatmap of Price vs. Market Per Station

Average ¢/L each station charges above (red) or below (green) the median of all prices posted the same day. Comparing to the same day's market makes stations fair to compare even when their data covers different periods.

Price vs. Market by Brand

Each point is one station's average gap to the same day's market median. Brands with fewer than 3 stations are grouped as Other. The Kruskal-Wallis p-value says how likely gaps this large would be if brands didn't really differ.

How Prices Change

First panel: every station price change, compared with that station's previous price. Second panel: after the market median moves by 3¢ or more in a day, the share of stations that have followed it (moved 2¢ or more the same way) over the next 48 hours. A faster red curve means prices rise quickly but fall slowly ("rockets and feathers").

Who Moves First

For each market move, the stations that followed are ranked by how soon they changed price (0 = first, 100 = last), then averaged by brand.
